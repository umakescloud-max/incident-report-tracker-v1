State incident reports get retyped by hand, tracked in a spreadsheet, and rejected for one missing time stamp with hours left on the clock.

I built a concept prototype for senior living compliance teams, built around that morning scramble.

It puts every open incident across five communities in one list, with the time left before each filing deadline.

It flags gaps early. One example is a fall report missing the physician notification time, shown with a live countdown to the deadline.

It shows a drafted report, but nothing goes to the state until a person reads it and clicks approve.
